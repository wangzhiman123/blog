This site collects working notes on **Chinese civil litigation and administrative
litigation** — appeal procedures, litigation-fee reduction and exemption, deemed
withdrawal, and retrial applications — written from a practitioner's point of view.

Most articles are currently written in **Chinese**. Use the language switch in the
header to return to the Chinese site, where the full archive lives.

If you are looking for a specific topic, the Chinese archive is organised by
category and tag; case citations are quoted from published judgments.
